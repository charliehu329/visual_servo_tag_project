Interpretation: sigma_min is the smallest singular value of the camera-frame 6x7 Jacobian. The solver scales qdot_raw by singularity_scale = smoothstep(sigma_min) between sigma_stop and sigma_warn, and returns zero below sigma_stop, so a small sigma_min slows or freezes the task no matter how large the image error is.
sigma_min <= 0 samples mean the joint feedback timed out and J_camera was zeroed, not a real singularity.
